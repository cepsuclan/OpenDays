[II] The Digital Detective's Journey

Mastering the fundamentals of Incident Response (IR) means you become an asset capable of building resilient systems, ensuring trust (GDPR, HIPAA), and saving the day when a breach occurs.

STAGE 1: Network Analysis Warm-up

The Mission: Your first mission is a straightforward, easily traceable attack. This is your introduction to digital network analysis, where you learn the foundational skill of reading and interpreting log data.

STAGE 2: Complex Investigation

The Mission: The threats get smarter. Tackle a scenario involving sophisticated, stealth-focused techniques often seen in Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs). The data is messier and tracks have been covered.

STAGE 3: Command & Control

The Mission: This is the ultimate test—you become the Incident Commander. The outcome depends entirely on your critical, time-sensitive decisions.

Sim Mechanic: Tracks performance via Organizational Health/Score. Good choices (isolation) improve score; bad choices (premature public statements) reduce it.
